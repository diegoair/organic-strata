Blob Boundary

Organica exploration — a mask shape morphs between presets (GSAP MorphSVGPlugin, already vendored for Soul); a fixed dot lattice is classified live every frame against the mask's own current geometry (SVGGeometryElement.isPointInFill) — dots deep inside stay small and separate, dots straddling the boundary enlarge and overlap into a gooey fringe, dots outside are hidden. No blur/goo filter — the lumpy edge is just plain same-colour circles overlapping. Reference: giuseppelaezza.com/playground.
