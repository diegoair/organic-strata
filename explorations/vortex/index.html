Vortex

Hover the 5-colour line at the centre to begin — it eases into the vortex's own first frame, then generation grows outward from there, slowly at first. Once the spiral has fully formed, click + drag to orbit the camera on all three axes (hold Shift while dragging for roll). At rest it's just the original 5-segment shape, motionless; hovering grows it into the full vortex: independent colour segments spread across a conical helix, each following the spiral on average while drifting with its own coherent-noise/spin/orbit. True-perspective projection.

Each segment is its own independent element — not welded into a rigid 5-piece line — evenly spread across "Coverage" (the fraction of the small→large loop populated; 1.0 = the whole spiral, no gaps). Colour cycles through the 5-colour palette by index, so neighbours differ but the sequence still reads as repeating groups of 5 along the spiral. Depth-sorted every frame (farthest first) so nearer segments correctly occlude farther ones.

At Noise amount 0, every segment sits exactly on the ideal spiral (the previous, perfectly rigid behaviour). Above 0, each segment gets its OWN 3D coherent-noise offset (seeded by its own index, so no two segments move alike) added on top of its spiral position — the spiral is what the segments follow on AVERAGE, not a rigid track they're welded to. Each segment's tangent is recomputed from its own noise-perturbed path, so it also rotates independently, not just drifts.

Spin — each segment also rotates on its OWN axis, on top of (not instead of) following the path's tangent direction — its own rate is a fixed per-index multiple of "Spin speed" (seeded by index, so segments spin at genuinely different rates/directions, some clockwise, some counter, some barely at all). Orbit — each segment additionally circles a small local point near its own spiral position (its own radius scaled by "Orbit radius", its own rate a per-index multiple of "Orbit speed") — "rotating around other random points", not just the shared spiral axis. Both are 0 by default (no change from plain path-following); dial in either independently.
