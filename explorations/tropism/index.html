Tropism — three tests

Each test is a small Component drawn here, outside FVS, to see what the idea does before anything is planned in the tool. The Element is the letter F (asymmetric, so every turn and flip shows) or a two-layer mark.

A signal per cell — a value 0–1 read under each cell’s centre — decides its turn, size or ink. Today only a rule decides; here a photo or a field does, and the grid becomes a picture made of Elements.

Drives

Turn: the level picks 0 · 90 · 180 · 270°. Size: dark = large. Ink: the level picks one of three inks.

Walks — two more orders for Step turn: each cell turns a quarter more than the one before it along the walk. Today the walk is reading order; Spiral goes round from the edge to the centre, Snake goes back and forth row by row.

Show

Offset and Stretch per cell — the rule’s class (Checkerboard A / B, a row, a column) moves or stretches each cell by a % of the cell. Glide is Mirror’s missing symmetry: every other row flipped and shifted half a cell (the brick of a woven pattern).

Layers follow the rule — a two-layer Element (a quarter ring under a dot). The rule’s class decides, per cell, which layers show, or whether only the top layer turns. Today every layer always shows and turns with the cell.
