Membrane

Organica exploration — ported from generative-design's P_2_2_3_02 ("form morphing process by connected random agents", Groß/Bohnacker/Laub/Lazzeroni, Generative Gestaltung, Apache 2.0). A ring of points each takes its own small independent random step every frame; drawn as one smooth curve through all of them, on a canvas that's never cleared, the accumulating strokes read as a soft membrane breathing and drifting — Organica's own "not drawn, allowed to happen" gesture, one step removed from the drop mark.

Resolution is how many points make up the form; Init radius sets the size of a freshly spawned one. Circle closes the curve into a loop; Line leaves it open, stretched between two random endpoints.

Motion
2.0
0.010

Step size is how far each point can wander in one frame — small values breathe gently, large values tear the form apart into chaos. Float speed is how eagerly the whole form drifts toward the mouse (0 = anchored in place). Click anywhere on the canvas to spawn a fresh form at that point.

0.75

Fill mode washes a new random translucent fill under the stroke every single frame — since the canvas never clears, this is what turns the drifting outline into dense, layered membrane tissue instead of a bare wireframe.
