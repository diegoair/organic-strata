Motion for the 404 and the sign-in

One Element in every cell of a grid, each cell's turn set by a rule. The 404 uses both proposals, full screen, in the colours of a palette from the library: every visit shows the other one and the next palette. The frames are live — open one to see it full size.

404 · Arrival — palette “Stimulating”Open

Element: Arc truchet · rule: Checkerboard 0° / 90°, the ink alternating with the turn. The grid arrives the way a Symbol does after Generate (blur → contrast → sharp, cells in a scattered order). The numerals are the cells that never finish arriving; the rest keeps changing, one cell at a time.

404 · Rules — palette “Violet 06”Open

Element: Arc (a quarter ring). Every cell turns in quarter turns as the grid moves through the named rules — Radial → Checkerboard → Pinwheel → Identity, one every 6 s. The numerals are the cells that never obey the rule, in the palette's strongest ink.

Colour: the ground is the palette colour that leaves the others the most contrast; an ink keeps its hue and moves along its own shade scale only as far as it must (1.8:1 for the pattern, 3:1 for the numerals in Rules). Palettes come from the library — the ones saved in TuneSutra, then the three built-in combinations. Open the 404 as a visitor gets it and reload to see it alternate; ?p=a|b and ?pal=0… pin a motion and a palette. The module is shared/fvs-field.js (Organica.fvsField); the page is the real /404.html.
