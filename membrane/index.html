Resolution is how many points make up the form; Init radius sets a freshly spawned one's size. Circle closes the curve into a loop; Line leaves it open; Point collapses the form to a single dot — no curve, driven by any Movement pattern below. Fill (Ink section) has no effect in Point mode. Seed shape (Circle only, Procedural seed) reshapes that ring — Noise blob perturbs the radius with organic noise, Cluster gives each point its own heavy random radius for a lumpy silhouette.

Single ink is the only mode Fill applies to and the only mode the Ink swatch drives directly — Rainbow cycles the spectrum once around the form/path, Sample from image reads live colour from the loaded image, RMX maps up to 5 colours across the form/path by the chosen mapping (Camo Turing's own Tone/Posterize/Random/Tone+Random, ported from its GLSL to plain JS since Membrane has no shader). Background is the canvas's own ground, independent of Color mode — Camo Turing's Ink/Paper pairing, split here between Palette (Ink) and the canvas's own fill (Background), since Membrane draws marks over a ground rather than a duotone field.
