Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026 · Private beta

Organica is a set of creative tools for designers and artists, currently in a private, invite-only beta. This policy explains what personal data the service processes, why, on what legal basis, and the rights you have.

Who is responsible (data controller)

[controller name / entity], [registered address] — [VAT / REA number, if a registered business]. Contact for any privacy matter: [contact email]. There is no Data Protection Officer; the contact above handles all requests.

What we collect and why

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name, and profile picture, via our authentication provider Supabase Auth. Used only to identify your account and keep you signed in. Your Google profile picture is loaded directly from Google's servers when it is displayed.
  • Your saved work. The seeds, palettes, presets, grids, and tool settings you choose to save. Stored so they are available on any device where you sign in.
  • Local browser storage. Your browser keeps a local copy of your saved work and a sign-in token so the tools load fast and work offline. Specifically: a session token under organica.auth.token, and per-tool settings under organica.<tool>.* keys. This is browser localStorage, not cookies, and stays on your device except when syncing your saved work to your account.
  • Aggregate analytics. Anonymous, cookieless page-view counts via Vercel Web Analytics, on the homepage only. No profile is built and nothing is linked to your account.

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the tools to you as a signed-up user: your account and your saved work.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the service secure and understanding aggregate usage; balanced against your rights, and limited to cookieless, non-identifying analytics.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Account data and saved work sit in a Postgres database provided by Supabase, hosted in the EU (eu-west-1, Ireland). Every account's saved work is isolated at the database level so no other user can read it. Sub-processors:

ProviderRoleLocation
SupabaseDatabase & authenticationEU (Ireland)
VercelWebsite hosting & homepage analyticsGlobal edge; company in the US
GoogleSign-in (OAuth) & profile pictureUS

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising.

International transfers

Sign-in with Google and the Google profile picture involve a transfer of data to the United States, carried out under Google's Standard Contractual Clauses and its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Vercel likewise relies on Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase processing stays within the EU.

Retention

Account data and saved work are kept while your account exists. If you delete your account (see below) everything associated with it is removed immediately and permanently. Aggregate analytics are not tied to you and are retained by Vercel per their own policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise any of these, email the contact above; we respond within 30 days. You can delete your account and all associated data yourself at any time from the account menu in the app header ("Delete my account"). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. The sign-in token and your tool settings live in your browser's localStorage and are strictly necessary to run the service. Analytics on the homepage are cookieless.

Automated decision-making

None. Nothing about you is decided automatically.

Children

Organica is a professional tool and is not directed at anyone under 18.
