Organica/ Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026 · Private beta

Organica is a set of creative tools for designers and artists, currently in a private, invite-only beta. This policy explains what data the service handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google, through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We use these only to identify your account and keep you signed in.
  • Your saved work. The seeds, palettes, presets, grids, and tool settings you choose to save. We store these so they are available on any device where you sign in.
  • Local browser storage. Your browser keeps a local copy of your saved work and your sign-in session, so the tools load fast and keep working offline. This never leaves your device except to sync with your account.
  • Aggregate analytics. Anonymous, cookie-less page-view counts via Vercel Web Analytics. This does not build a profile of you and is not linked to your account.

Where it is stored

Account data and saved work are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase. The site itself is hosted by Vercel. Sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase Auth.

How it is used and shared

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising. Your data is processed only by the infrastructure providers needed to run the service — Google (sign-in), Supabase (database and auth), and Vercel (hosting) — and only for that purpose. Every account's saved work is isolated by row-level security so no other user can read it.

Cookies

We use a single first-party token in your browser to keep you signed in. There are no third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and everything associated with it, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the beta develops. The date above reflects the most recent change.
