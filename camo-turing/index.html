Solid: the field's own threshold decides ink vs paper, same as Duotone everywhere else in Organica. Adaptive: a smooth blend between Ink and Paper by the field's own value, no hard edge. RMX: up to 5 colours mapped across the field — Tone blends them like a gradient, Posterize bands them, Random/Tone+Random add a hashed jitter (a texture, not per-blob colour — the field is continuous, not discrete marks like Pollen's stipple points).
