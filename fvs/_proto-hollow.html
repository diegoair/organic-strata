Hollow — one for every shape (prototype)

Today six shapes each have their own Outline (hollow): the shape scaled toward its centre and cut out. That is an exact wall only for triangles and regular polygons. The generic Hollow keeps the part of the shape within a fixed distance of its outline — a wall of constant thickness on any shape, holes included. Wall = Thickness % of the shape's inradius, the same unit the Outline sliders use.

Wall = measured distance from the inner edge to the outline, min–max, as a multiple of the target (1.00–1.00 is a perfectly even wall). Differs = share of the shape's pixels that the two hollows paint differently.
